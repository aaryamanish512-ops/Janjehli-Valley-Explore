02 — FAITH & FOLKLORE

Shikari Mata
Temple

Located high in the mountains of Mandi district, Shikari Devi Temple is known for its roofless shrine, mountain views and important place in local faith.

History & local traditions

Government tourism descriptions identify this as an ancient place of worship. Local traditions connect the shrine with the Pandavas and sage Markandeya, who is believed to have meditated here. These accounts are religious traditions, not independently verified historical dates.

The roofless shrine is one of the temple's most distinctive features. Different local stories explain it; these should be understood as folklore rather than proven historical explanations.

04 — PLAN YOUR VISIT

A thoughtful travel guide.

Getting there

Janjehli is accessible by road. Himachal Tourism lists it at around 85 km from Mandi; actual travel time varies. Confirm the route and road conditions locally.

When to visit

Clear spring, summer and autumn days may be suitable for sightseeing. Rain, snow, landslides and seasonal closures can affect access. Check conditions before leaving.

What to carry

Carry warm layers, sturdy footwear, water, snacks, a charged phone, necessary medicines and a bag for waste. Mobile coverage may be limited.

Mountain safety

Avoid travel during severe weather warnings. Do not trek alone. Check trail conditions, daylight, local guidance and applicable forest or sanctuary rules.

Before you leave:

Check current weather alerts, road status and local administration updates. This website does not provide live road or emergency information.
